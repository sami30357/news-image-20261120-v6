## THE JUDGE'S OWN CRITERIA (competition.yaml, read live — this is the find)

its day's news events you know happened before that date, LOSES to any that
does not"; (2) selection and breadth across regions and fields; (3) it reads —
a viewer who missed the day can tell what happened, each story clear from its
picture and short labels, nothing self-contradictory, implausible or known
false; (4) faithful — what happened, as reported, without hype, or speculation
shown as fact.
